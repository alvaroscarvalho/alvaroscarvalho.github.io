About

I'm a Senior SRE focused on reliability at scale, with a path from network engineering that gives me a systems-level view of infrastructure, from routing and DNS to what a runtime is doing under memory pressure.

Incident management sits at the core of how I work, end to end: fast troubleshooting and mitigation under pressure, then post-mortems and root-cause analysis that turn a bad night into something the team handles calmly next time. I've owned the tooling behind that too, building and managing incident platforms like PagerDuty and Incident.io, from base configuration to alert orchestration and multi-team escalation flows.

Around that, I've owned cross-team SLO and error-budget rollouts, and I build observability and alerting meant to drive action rather than add noise, backed by runbooks that guide on-call through the first minutes of an incident. The rest of my time goes into removing toil through automation and tuning application performance under real production load.

What I work on

Four areas where I spend most of my time.

Incident Management

Owner of PagerDuty and Incident.io: base configuration, alert orchestration, and multi-team escalation flows. End to end, from mitigation under pressure to post-mortems and RCA.

Observability

Prometheus stack, New Relic, and OpenSearch. Meaningful SLIs/SLOs and error budgets, and alerting tuned to cut noise so the pages that reach on-call actually matter.

Runtime Performance

Garbage-collection and heap tuning across JVM (ZGC, G1GC), Go, and Node.js for production services under memory pressure, cutting footprint and stopping OOM restarts.

Kubernetes & Cloud

Multi-region EKS with hands-on cluster management, on AWS with Terraform and CI/CD, partnering closely with DevOps teams.
